TL;DW settings

Run tldw serve in a terminal; it prints a token. Paste it here.

Used for summaries, key moments and questions. Sonnet finishes in roughly half the time and writes shorter summaries; Opus is more thorough. Applies to the Claude CLI backend.

Applies to Play key moments, not the written summary. Auto lets a dense video stay long and a padded one get cut hard; anything else is both a target and a hard cap on the total clip time.

Some videos have no subtitles or auto-captions at all. TL;DW can transcribe the audio on this machine with Whisper instead, which takes roughly a minute for every five and a half minutes of video and uses the CPU while it runs. The transcript is kept, so it only happens once per video. Needs pipx inject youtube-tldw faster-whisper.

Summaries, key-moment picking, speech and answers all keep running if you close the panel to go watch the video — you'll get a notice when a summary lands. Choose “Stop and discard” to cancel the work instead.

You can also switch the voice per-summary in the popup.

TL;DW is free and runs entirely on your own machine, on your own Claude plan. If it saves you time, sponsoring it on GitHub is welcome — never required.
